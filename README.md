### The Roadmap Structure
NeetCode functions as a step-by-step roadmap tailored for systematic algorithmic learning:

* **Graded Difficulty:** Problems progress naturally from fundamentals to advanced applications, allowing you to build intuition without hitting a steep difficulty wall.
* **Pattern-Based Organization:** Categorized by core patterns (Arrays & Hashing, Binary Search, Graphs, Dynamic Programming) to teach repeatable problem-solving strategies rather than memorization.
* **Logical Progression:** Acts as a prerequisite tree, ensuring you master foundational data structures before moving into complex algorithms.
